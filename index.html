Privacy Policy for Invoice Maker

Last Updated: May 20, 2026

Sajid AI Lab ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Invoice Maker mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information Collection and Use

A. Personal & Business Data

While using our Service, you may input or provide certain business and personal details required to generate invoices, such as:

  • Business details (Name, Address, Tax ID, Logo)
  • Contact details (Email address, Phone/WhatsApp number)
  • Client details and billing information
  • Digital Signatures created for authorization

All digital signatures and invoice details are processed locally on your device or included directly in generated PDF documents. We do not transfer or sell your signature data to external servers.

B. Usage, Diagnostic & Advertising Data

We work with third-party service providers (such as Google AdMob) to serve advertisements within the app. These services may collect certain device identifiers and non-personal diagnostic data automatically, including:

  • Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID - AD_ID)
  • IP address, network state, and general location data
  • App performance, usage statistics, and crash logs

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

Our app displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may use device identifiers, cookies, or similar technologies to personalize ads, serve relevant content, and analyze ad performance.

To learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use their services, please review Google's Advertising Privacy & Terms.

You can manage or opt-out of personalized ads through your device settings under Settings > Google > Ads.

3. Data Storage, Backup & Google Drive Integration

A. Local Storage

The primary function of Invoice Maker is to store your business records locally on your device. We do not host your invoices, client data, or financial items on our own company servers.

B. Google Drive Sync (User-Controlled Backup)

Our app provides an optional feature to backup and restore your data via your personal Google Drive account.

  • Restricted Access: If enabled, the app requests permission to access its own dedicated, restricted app-folder in your Google Drive.
  • Privacy: We do not have access to your Google password, personal files, or any other data in your Google Drive.
  • Google API Compliance: The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs strictly adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements.

4. Device Permissions

To provide full functionality, the App may request access to:

  • Camera & Photos: To allow you to attach business logos or receipt images to invoices.
  • Storage/Media Access: To export, render, and save PDF invoices to your phone's storage.
  • Internet & Network State: To facilitate Google Drive backup/restore actions and render advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): Used by Google AdMob to serve and personalize advertisements in accordance with Google Play Policy.

5. Security of Data

The security of your data is paramount. Since your data is primarily stored locally and synced through your personal Google account, your information benefits from your device's built-in security and Google's encryption standards. While we implement standard safety measures, no electronic storage method is 100% secure.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed to individuals under the age of 13 ("Children"). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Your Data Control and Rights

You maintain full control over your data:

  • Deletion: You can clear or delete all local records, invoices, and saved signatures within the app settings at any time.
  • Backup Management: You can remove or manage your backup files directly inside your Google Drive account or revoke app permissions via your Google Account Security settings.
  • Ad Opt-Out: You can reset or opt-out of personalized advertising in your Android system settings.
